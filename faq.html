Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ONE PEAK and the Viral Content Accelerator program.

What exactly is ONE PEAK?
ONE PEAK is a comprehensive 12-week accelerator program that teaches you the exact system for creating viral content consistently across all major platforms. It includes pre-recorded modules, live coaching calls, AI tools, and access to a private community of serious creators. We focus on practical, actionable strategies—not theory.
How much does the program cost?
The program is a one-time investment of $5,999. We also offer 0% financing options through our partners, allowing you to split the payment into manageable monthly installments. This includes lifetime access to all course materials, tools, and community features. There are no hidden fees or upsells.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
Yes! We offer a 30-day risk-free guarantee. If you complete the first two modules, attend the coaching calls, and don't feel you're getting value, we'll refund your full investment—no questions asked. We're confident in our program because it works.
How is the program delivered?
The program is delivered through our online learning platform. You get: (1) 12 pre-recorded video modules released weekly, (2) Live group coaching calls every Tuesday and Thursday, (3) Monthly 1-on-1 sessions with your assigned coach, and (4) 24/7 access to our private Discord community for daily support and networking.
Do I need prior content creation experience?
No prior experience is required! Our curriculum is designed for everyone from complete beginners to experienced creators looking to scale. We meet you where you are and provide personalized guidance based on your current level and goals.
How much time do I need to dedicate each week?
We recommend 5-7 hours per week: 2-3 hours for consuming course content, 1-2 hours for live coaching calls (recorded if you can't attend), and 2-3 hours for content creation and implementation. The more you put in, the faster you'll see results.
What platforms do you cover?
We cover all major short-form video platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Twitter/X. Our principles apply universally, but we provide platform-specific strategies for each. We also cover long-form YouTube and podcasting for advanced monetization.
Can I join if I'm outside the United States?
Absolutely! We have members from 48+ countries. All live coaching calls are recorded and available for replay. Our Discord community is active 24/7 across all time zones. The program is entirely online, so you can participate from anywhere in the world.
What results can I realistically expect?
Results vary based on your niche, effort, and consistency. However, our average student sees a 5-10x increase in views within the first 8 weeks. Many students achieve their first viral video (100K+ views) within the program. We have numerous case studies of students hitting millions of views. Check our Results page for real examples.
When does the next cohort start?
We run cohorts monthly, starting on the first Monday of each month. Once you enroll, you'll get immediate access to Module 1 and our community. Live coaching begins on the cohort start date. Spots are limited to ensure quality coaching ratios.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is here to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
